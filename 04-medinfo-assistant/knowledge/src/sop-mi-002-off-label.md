# SOP-MI-002: Handling unsolicited requests for off-label information

## 1 Purpose

To make sure that questions about uses of Larkmoor products outside the approved Prescribing Information are answered only by Medical Information, accurately and without promotion.

## 2 Definitions

- **On-label:** consistent with the indications, populations, doses, routes and administration described in the approved Prescribing Information.
- **Off-label:** any use outside the approved Prescribing Information, including other indications, other age groups, other doses or dosing schedules, and other methods of administration (for example crushing tablets when the labeling says to swallow them whole).
- **Unsolicited request:** a question asked on the healthcare professional's own initiative, without prompting by Larkmoor.

## 3 Field sales representatives

Field sales representatives must not discuss off-label use, even if asked directly. When a healthcare professional asks an off-label question, the representative must:

1. Say that they cannot discuss uses outside the approved labeling.
2. Offer to submit a Medical Information request on the healthcare professional's behalf.
3. Submit the request within 2 business days through the Medical Information portal or the Medical Information assistant, recording the healthcare professional's name, contact details and exact question, without rewording it.
4. Not suggest questions, and not deliver the written response themselves.

Representatives may share only the approved Prescribing Information.

## 4 Medical science liaisons

Medical science liaisons may respond to unsolicited off-label requests with balanced scientific information, provided the request is documented in the Medical Information system.

## 5 Medical Information

- Medical Information responds to off-label requests from healthcare professionals within 2 business days, using approved standard response letters where available.
- Responses are scientifically balanced, non-promotional and include the approved Prescribing Information.
- Questions from patients and caregivers about off-label use are not answered; Medical Information advises them to speak to their healthcare provider.

## 6 Records and audit

All off-label requests and responses are kept for at least 10 years. Medical Information audits a sample of field-submitted requests every quarter.

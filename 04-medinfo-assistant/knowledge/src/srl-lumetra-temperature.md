# Lumetra (pelvaglutide): temperature excursions and stability data

## Summary

- Labeled storage: refrigerate unused pens at 2°C to 8°C. After first use, pens may be kept for up to 56 days at room temperature up to 30°C or in a refrigerator (Prescribing Information section 16).
- Stability data show that unused pens remain within specification after a cumulative 21 days at up to 30°C, or after up to 48 hours at up to 37°C.
- A pen that has been frozen must be discarded, even if it has thawed.
- There are no data supporting use after excursions above 30°C once a pen has been opened.

## Stability data for unused pens

| Excursion | Result | Recommendation from Larkmoor |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 30°C, cumulative 21 days or less | Within specification | Pen may be used; return to the refrigerator and start the 56-day in-use period at first use |
| Up to 30°C, more than 21 days cumulative | Not tested | Do not use |
| 30°C to 37°C, 48 hours or less | Within specification | Pen may be used; treat it as an in-use pen (56 days from the excursion) |
| Above 37°C, any duration | Not tested | Do not use |
| Frozen (0°C or below), any duration | Particle formation observed | Do not use, even after thawing |

## Pens that have been opened

The labeled in-use period is 56 days at up to 30°C. No stability data are available for opened pens exposed to temperatures above 30°C. Larkmoor recommends discarding them.

## Reporting

Temperature excursions at pharmacies or wholesalers should also be reported to Larkmoor Quality Assurance as a product complaint if the product appears damaged.

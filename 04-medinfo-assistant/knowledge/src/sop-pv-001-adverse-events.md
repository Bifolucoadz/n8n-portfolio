# SOP-PV-001: Adverse event intake and reporting

## 1 Purpose

This procedure describes how Larkmoor Therapeutics employees and contractors recognize, record and forward adverse events and special situations involving Larkmoor products, so that Pharmacovigilance can meet regulatory reporting timelines.

## 2 Scope

All employees, contractors and vendors who interact with healthcare professionals, patients or the public, including field sales, medical science liaisons, Medical Information, customer service and digital channels such as the Medical Information assistant.

## 3 Definitions

- **Adverse event (AE):** any untoward medical occurrence in a patient given a Larkmoor product, whether or not it is considered related to the product.
- **Serious adverse event (SAE):** an AE that results in death, is life-threatening, requires or prolongs hospitalization, results in persistent or significant disability, is a congenital anomaly, or is an important medical event.
- **Special situations:** exposure during pregnancy or breastfeeding, overdose, medication error, misuse or abuse, off-label use, lack of efficacy, occupational exposure and suspected transmission of an infectious agent. Special situations must be reported even when no adverse event occurred.
- **Day 0:** the date on which any Larkmoor employee, contractor or digital channel first receives the information.

## 4 Minimum information for a valid case

A case is valid when it has all four of the following:

1. An identifiable patient (for example initials, age, sex or date of birth; a name is not required).
2. An identifiable reporter (name and a way to contact them, or their profession).
3. A suspect Larkmoor product.
4. An adverse event or special situation.

Forward the information even if one or more elements are missing. Pharmacovigilance completes the case through follow-up.

## 5 Timelines

| Situation | Forward to Pharmacovigilance |
|---|---|
| Any adverse event or special situation | Within 1 business day (24 hours) of Day 0 |
| Serious adverse event, including any death | Same day by phone to the Pharmacovigilance hotline, then the intake form |
| Follow-up information on an existing case | Within 1 business day of receipt |

## 6 How to report

- Pharmacovigilance intake form on the intranet, or email pv-intake@larkmoor.example.
- Pharmacovigilance hotline (24 hours a day, 7 days a week): 1-800-555-0177.
- Adverse events received through the Medical Information assistant are recorded automatically and alerted to Pharmacovigilance. The date of the conversation is Day 0.

## 7 Responsibilities of the person receiving the report

- Record the reporter's own words. Do not interpret, summarize the medical meaning or assess whether the product caused the event.
- Ask for the reporter's contact details so that Pharmacovigilance can follow up.
- Do not give medical advice. Refer patients to their healthcare provider; in an emergency, tell them to call emergency services.
- Do not contact the patient directly for follow-up. Pharmacovigilance performs all follow-up.
- If the report also describes a product quality problem, also follow SOP-QA-004.

## 8 Reconciliation

Pharmacovigilance reconciles the safety database each month with Medical Information requests, product complaints and the Medical Information assistant's case log.

## 9 Training

All employees in scope complete adverse event reporting training when they join and every year after that.

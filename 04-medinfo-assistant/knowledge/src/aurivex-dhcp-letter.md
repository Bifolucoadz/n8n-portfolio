# IMPORTANT PRESCRIBING INFORMATION

August 15, 2026

Subject: Change in the water volume used to reconstitute Aurivex (cefovatrin) for oral suspension 250 mg/5 mL, 100 mL bottle (NDC 99107-425-10)

Dear Healthcare Provider,

Larkmoor Therapeutics is introducing a new bottle for Aurivex 250 mg/5 mL in the 100 mL size. The new bottle has a different powder fill and headspace. As a result, the volume of water needed to reconstitute it has changed.

## What has changed

- Lots AV26-0412 and later (new bottle): add 62 mL of water in two portions.
- Lots AV26-0411 and earlier (previous bottle): continue to add 66 mL of water, as stated in the Prescribing Information (section 2.4).
- The correct volume is printed on each bottle label. Always follow the volume on the bottle being reconstituted.

The 125 mg/5 mL strength and the 250 mg/5 mL 60 mL bottle are not affected.

## Why this matters

Adding 66 mL of water to a new-bottle lot gives a suspension of about 235 mg/5 mL, about 6% weaker than labeled. Pharmacists should check the lot number before reconstituting.

## What to do

- Check the lot number on the carton or bottle before adding water.
- Update pharmacy reconstitution instructions and labels for the 100 mL bottle.
- The Prescribing Information will be updated at its next revision.

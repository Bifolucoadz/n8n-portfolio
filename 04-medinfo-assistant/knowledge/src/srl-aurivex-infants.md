# Aurivex (cefovatrin): use in infants younger than 3 months

## Summary

- Aurivex is approved for pediatric patients 3 months to 12 years of age. Safety and effectiveness in patients younger than 3 months have not been established (Prescribing Information section 8.4).
- Limited pharmacokinetic data in 18 infants aged 4 to 12 weeks showed slower clearance and a longer half-life than in older children.
- Larkmoor Therapeutics cannot provide a dosing recommendation for infants younger than 3 months.

## Pharmacokinetic data

An open-label, single-dose study (LA-PED-04) enrolled 18 infants aged 4 to 12 weeks who were receiving antibacterial treatment for a suspected bacterial infection. Each infant received one dose of cefovatrin 7 mg/kg as oral suspension.

| Parameter | Infants 4 to 12 weeks (n=18) | Children 6 months to 12 years (historical) |
|---|---|---|
| Elimination half-life | 2.9 hours | 1.6 hours |
| Apparent clearance | About 40% lower | Reference |
| Time to peak concentration | 2.1 hours | 1.4 hours |

No serious adverse events were reported after the single dose. Two infants had loose stools within 48 hours. The study was not designed to assess efficacy or multiple-dose safety.

## Neonates

No data are available in neonates younger than 4 weeks of age.

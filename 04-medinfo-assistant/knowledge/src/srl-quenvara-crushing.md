# Quenvara (davorixaban): crushing tablets and administration through feeding tubes

## Summary

- The Prescribing Information instructs patients to swallow Quenvara tablets whole with water (section 2.7). Crushed administration is not addressed in the labeling.
- In vitro, crushed Quenvara 5 mg tablets suspended in water or 5% dextrose were stable for 4 hours and passed through an 8 French nasogastric tube with 98.6% recovery.
- In healthy volunteers, a crushed 5 mg tablet given by nasogastric tube gave exposure similar to the whole tablet swallowed.
- No data are available for post-pyloric (nasojejunal or jejunostomy) tubes. Absorption may be reduced when davorixaban is delivered beyond the stomach.

## In vitro data

Crushed 5 mg tablets were suspended in 60 mL of water or 5% dextrose in water (D5W). Davorixaban content remained between 98% and 101% of the initial value for 4 hours at room temperature. After passage through an 8 French polyvinyl chloride nasogastric tube followed by a 20 mL water flush, recovery was 98.6%.

## Pharmacokinetic study

In an open-label crossover study, 24 healthy volunteers received a single 5 mg dose as a whole tablet swallowed or as a crushed tablet suspended in 60 mL water and given through a nasogastric tube.

| Parameter (crushed via NG tube vs whole tablet) | Geometric mean ratio | 90% confidence interval |
|---|---|---|
| AUC | 0.97 | 0.91 to 1.03 |
| Cmax | 1.08 | 0.99 to 1.18 |

## Practical points reported in the study protocol

- Crush the tablet to a fine powder with a mortar and pestle.
- Suspend in 60 mL of water or D5W and give within 4 hours.
- Flush the tube with at least 20 mL of water afterwards.

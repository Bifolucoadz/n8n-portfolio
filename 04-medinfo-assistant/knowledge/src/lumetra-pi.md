# LUMETRA (pelvaglutide) injection, for subcutaneous use

## BOXED WARNING

**WARNING: RISK OF THYROID C-CELL TUMORS**

- In rodents, pelvaglutide causes dose-dependent thyroid C-cell tumors. It is unknown whether Lumetra causes thyroid C-cell tumors, including medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC), in humans (5.1).
- Lumetra is contraindicated in patients with a personal or family history of MTC or in patients with Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2) (4).

## 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Lumetra is a glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonist indicated as an adjunct to diet and exercise to improve glycemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus.

Limitations of use:

- Not for use in patients with type 1 diabetes mellitus or for the treatment of diabetic ketoacidosis.
- Not studied in patients with a history of pancreatitis.
- Not indicated for chronic weight management.

## 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

### 2.1 Recommended dosage

| Step | Dose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Initiation | 0.5 mg once weekly | 4 weeks (not effective for glycemic control) |
| Maintenance | 1 mg once weekly | At least 4 weeks before any increase |
| Additional glycemic control | 2 mg once weekly | Maximum recommended dose |

Administer once weekly on the same day each week, at any time of day, with or without meals.

### 2.2 Missed dose

If a dose is missed, administer it as soon as possible within 4 days (96 hours) after the missed dose. If more than 4 days have passed, skip the missed dose and give the next dose on the regularly scheduled day.

### 2.3 Changing the weekly injection day

The day of weekly administration can be changed if the time between two doses is at least 3 days (72 hours).

### 2.4 Administration

- Inject subcutaneously in the abdomen, thigh or upper arm. Rotate injection sites with each dose.
- Use a new needle for each injection. Never share a Lumetra pen between patients, even if the needle is changed.
- Inspect visually before use: the solution should be clear and colorless. Do not use if particles or discoloration are seen.
- When using Lumetra with insulin, give separate injections; never mix them.

## 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Injection: clear, colorless solution in single-patient-use prefilled pens. Each pen delivers 4 doses.

- Pen delivering 0.5 mg per injection (blue label)
- Pen delivering 1 mg per injection (green label)
- Pen delivering 2 mg per injection (purple label)

## 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

- Personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2.
- Serious hypersensitivity reaction to pelvaglutide or any of the excipients.

## 5 WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS

### 5.1 Risk of thyroid C-cell tumors

Counsel patients about the potential risk of MTC and the symptoms of thyroid tumors (a mass in the neck, dysphagia, dyspnea, persistent hoarseness).

### 5.2 Acute pancreatitis

Acute pancreatitis, including fatal and non-fatal hemorrhagic or necrotizing pancreatitis, has been observed with GLP-1 receptor agonists. If pancreatitis is suspected, discontinue Lumetra and do not restart if confirmed.

### 5.3 Hypoglycemia with insulin secretagogues or insulin

Patients receiving Lumetra with an insulin secretagogue (such as a sulfonylurea) or insulin have an increased risk of hypoglycemia. Consider lowering the dose of the sulfonylurea or insulin when starting Lumetra.

### 5.4 Acute kidney injury

Acute kidney injury has been reported, usually in patients with nausea, vomiting or diarrhea leading to dehydration. Monitor renal function in patients reporting severe gastrointestinal adverse reactions.

### 5.5 Diabetic retinopathy complications

Rapid improvement in glucose control has been associated with a temporary worsening of diabetic retinopathy. Monitor patients with a history of diabetic retinopathy.

### 5.6 Acute gallbladder disease

Cholelithiasis was reported in 1.4% of Lumetra-treated patients and 0.6% of placebo-treated patients. If cholecystitis is suspected, gallbladder studies are indicated.

### 6.1 Clinical trials experience

| Adverse reaction | Placebo (N=592) % | Lumetra 1 mg (N=601) % | Lumetra 2 mg (N=598) % |

| Nausea | 6.1 | 14.7 | 18.2 |
| Diarrhea | 5.4 | 8.6 | 10.1 |

Gastrointestinal adverse reactions were most frequent during dose escalation and decreased over time. Injection site reactions occurred in 2.0% of Lumetra-treated patients. Discontinuation because of gastrointestinal adverse reactions occurred in 3.9% of patients on 2 mg and 0.8% on placebo.

## 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

### 7.1 Oral medications

Lumetra delays gastric emptying and may affect the absorption of oral medications taken at the same time. Monitor drugs with a narrow therapeutic index, such as warfarin, when starting Lumetra.

### 7.2 Insulin secretagogues and insulin

See 5.3.

## 8 USE IN SPECIFIC POPULATIONS

### 8.1 Pregnancy

Limited data in pregnant women are insufficient to determine a drug-associated risk. Discontinue Lumetra at least 2 months before a planned pregnancy because of the long washout period of pelvaglutide.

### 8.2 Lactation

There are no data on the presence of pelvaglutide in human milk. Consider the benefits of breastfeeding together with the mother's clinical need for Lumetra.

### 8.4 Pediatric use

Safety and effectiveness have not been established in patients younger than 18 years.

### 8.6 Renal impairment

No dose adjustment is recommended for patients with renal impairment. Monitor renal function in patients with severe gastrointestinal adverse reactions (5.4).

### 8.7 Hepatic impairment

No dose adjustment is recommended for patients with hepatic impairment.

## 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

In the 40-week PELVA-2 trial, HbA1c fell from a baseline of 8.2% by 1.4 percentage points with Lumetra 1 mg and 1.8 percentage points with Lumetra 2 mg, compared with 0.3 percentage points with placebo.

Storage:

- Before first use: store in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F). Do not freeze. Do not use a pen that has been frozen.
- After first use: the pen can be stored for up to 56 days at room temperature up to 30°C (86°F) or in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C.
- Keep the pen cap on when not in use to protect from light. Remove the needle after each injection and store the pen without a needle attached.
- Discard the pen 56 days after first use, even if it still contains medicine.

## 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

- Inform patients of the potential risk of thyroid C-cell tumors and the symptoms to report.
- Advise patients to stop Lumetra and contact their healthcare provider if they have severe abdominal pain that does not go away, with or without vomiting.
- Advise patients to drink fluids to avoid dehydration if they have nausea, vomiting or diarrhea.

# SOP-QA-004: Product complaint intake

## 1 Purpose

To capture every product complaint about a Larkmoor product completely and forward it to Quality Assurance in time for investigation.

## 2 What counts as a product complaint

Any communication alleging a problem with the identity, quality, strength, purity, durability, reliability, safety, effectiveness or performance of a product after release. Examples:

- A Lumetra pen that does not dispense, leaks, or whose dose counter does not move.
- Broken, chipped or discolored Quenvara tablets, or the wrong number of tablets in a bottle.
- Aurivex powder that is caked or will not dissolve, or a suspension with an unusual color or smell.
- Damaged, missing or illegible labels, or tampered packaging.
- Suspected counterfeit product.

If the complaint also involves an adverse event (for example, a missed dose because a pen failed, followed by high blood sugar), it is both a product complaint and an adverse event. Follow this SOP and SOP-PV-001.

## 3 Information to collect

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Product and strength | For example Lumetra 1 mg pen |
| Lot number and expiry date | Printed on the carton and the pen, bottle or blister |
| Description of the problem | In the reporter's words |
| Whether a sample is available | Ask the reporter to keep the product and packaging |
| Reporter contact details | Name, phone or email |

## 4 Timelines and routing

- Forward every complaint to Quality Assurance within 1 business day: qa-complaints@larkmoor.example or 1-800-555-0166.
- Suspected counterfeit product or tampering: phone Quality Assurance immediately.
- Quality Assurance sends a prepaid return kit for the sample within 3 business days.

## 5 Do not

- Do not ask the reporter to throw the product away.
- Do not promise a replacement or refund; Quality Assurance decides after investigation.

## About Copperline Auto Repair

Copperline Auto Repair is an independent, family-run repair shop in Allston, Boston, open since
2009. Our technicians are ASE-certified. We have six service bays and work on most cars, SUVs,
minivans and light trucks, including hybrids and electric vehicles (see the hybrid and EV section
for what we do on those).

## Booking an appointment

- Book through the website chat, by phone or at the front desk.
- An appointment is a drop-off time. Please arrive at your booked time; most jobs are finished the
  same day and we text you when your car is ready.
- You can book up to 14 days ahead. Same-day bookings need at least 2 hours' notice.
- Bring your keys and, for a state inspection, your vehicle registration.
- To change or cancel, call us or reply to your confirmation email at least 4 hours before your
  appointment. There is no cancellation fee.

## Walk-ins

We take walk-ins for oil changes, tire rotations, battery tests and Massachusetts state
inspections. Booked customers go first, so a walk-in can wait up to 2 hours at busy times. For
anything else, please book.

## Waiting at the shop

You are welcome to wait for jobs that take under 90 minutes, such as an oil change, tire rotation,
battery replacement or state inspection. The waiting room has free Wi-Fi, coffee and phone
charging.

- Work not listed is charged at our labor rate of $145 per hour, after you approve a written

## Massachusetts state inspection

- $35, the fee set by the state.
- We are a licensed Massachusetts inspection station.
- Bring your vehicle registration. Walk-ins welcome; the last inspection of the day starts 30
  minutes before closing.
- If your car fails, we explain why and can quote the repair. You have 60 days for a free
  re-test at the same station.

## Pre-purchase inspection

Buying a used car? We inspect it for $159 and give you a written report covering the engine,
brakes, suspension, tires, fluids, leaks and any warning codes. Takes about 90 minutes. The seller
needs to bring the car to us.

## Hybrid and electric vehicles

- We service hybrids (Toyota, Lexus, Honda, Ford, Hyundai and Kia) and electric vehicles for tires,
  brakes, suspension, alignment, the 12-volt battery, cabin filters, wipers and air conditioning.
- We do not repair high-voltage battery packs or electric drive motors; those go to the dealer.

## Vehicles we don't service

- Exotic and high-performance cars (for example Ferrari, Lamborghini and McLaren).
- Heavy-duty trucks over 14,000 lb, RVs and motorcycles.
- Diesel engine repairs. We can still do tires, brakes and alignment on diesel vehicles.

## Safety: when not to drive

Stop driving as soon as it is safe, and do not drive to the shop, if:
- the brake pedal sinks to the floor, the brakes grind loudly, or the car pulls hard when braking;
- the temperature gauge is in the red or steam comes from under the hood;
- the check-engine light is flashing;
- you see smoke, smell burning or smell fuel;
- the steering suddenly becomes very heavy or loose;
- there's a loud bang followed by a loss of power.

Call our towing partner, available 24/7, on (617) 555-0199. If anyone is hurt or in danger, call 911.
Cars towed to us get $25 off the diagnostic.
